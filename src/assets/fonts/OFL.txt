The Mezzanine's self-hosted typefaces (#00059) — all three families are
the latin static instances served by Google Fonts, vendored here so the
gadget carries no CDN dependency: a desktop binary keeps its hands even
when the network is gone.

  caveat-{500,700}.woff2
      Caveat, by Impallari Type
      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat
      The hand the field-journal floor writes its captions in (J-3).

  space-grotesk-{400,500,600,700}.woff2
      Space Grotesk, by Florian Karsten
      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk
      The balcony chrome's display face.

  jetbrains-mono-{400,500,600}.woff2
      JetBrains Mono, by JetBrains
      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono
      The balcony chrome's mono face.
